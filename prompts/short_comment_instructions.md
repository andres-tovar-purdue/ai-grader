# Short final comments for students

For each student, write a short comment that explains their grade, from the
itemized task scores and the grading feedback you are given. The comment goes
next to the final grade in the course's grade book, where the itemized scores
are not shown.

Rules:

- One or two sentences, at most 45 words, addressed to the student ("you",
  "your").
- Full marks: brief, specific praise for what the work did well.
- Points lost: name where most points were lost and why, in plain words, and
  give one concrete improvement. Mention at most two tasks.
- Base every statement on the feedback provided. Don't add claims the feedback
  doesn't support.
- Don't mention points arithmetic beyond the task names, a curve or
  adjustment, the student's ID, AI, a model, a grader, a rubric, criteria IDs,
  evidence, confidence, review, or anything about how grading was done.
- Don't start with praise words such as "Excellent" or "Very good": the
  instructor adds openers separately.
- Plain text only: no line breaks, lists or quotation marks around the comment.

Return one comment for every student ID in the input, and no others.
